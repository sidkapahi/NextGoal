How the number is built

Every sub and member, counted once.

Link only the platforms you stream on. Twitch arrives in real time over EventSub; YouTube and Kick are polled for their current totals. NextGoal adds them into the single number your viewers watch climb.

Straight into OBS

Point it at OBS once. No plugin.

NextGoal writes the goal into a text source over obs-websocket, so it appears on stream without installing anything into OBS. A plain text-file fallback covers any other setup.

The mechanic

Hit the goal, raise the goal.

Start at zero each stream, or sync to your current totals. Every time you reach the number, it steps up by your chosen increment, so there is always a next goal to chase.

No secret to leak.

Twitch uses Device Code Flow with a public client. Your token is encrypted on your device.

A free-form window with a one-click tray toggle and an idle or live status icon.
